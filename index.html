ZOMBRYX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Zombryx saves your progress on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Summary

Zombryx is a zombie survival game made by an independent developer. The game does not collect, sell or share personal data. Everything you do in the game is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, records, coins, unlocked items, achievements and settings (language, sound, controls, graphics) in a file inside the app's private storage. This file never leaves your device and the developer cannot access it.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your unlocked achievements and your scores (best scores, best wave, zombies killed and story stars) to Google so they appear in your Play Games profile and leaderboards. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can play the whole game without signing in.

Updates

The game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available so it can offer to update from inside the app. This check is done by Google Play on your device and no personal data is sent to the developer.

What Zombryx does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat and no account of its own. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Zombryx contains cartoon violence against zombies and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all your progress. Data in Google Play Games can be managed or deleted from the Play Games app or your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
